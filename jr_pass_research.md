## 2. JR Pass Options

### Option A: JR West Kansai Area Pass (WEST QR)

| Duration | Adult | Child |
|----------|-------|-------|
| 1-day | ¥2,700 | ¥1,350 |
| 2-day | ¥4,700 | ¥2,350 |
| 3-day | ¥5,500 | ¥2,750 |
| 4-day | ¥6,700 | ¥3,350 |

**Coverage:**
- Non-reserved seats on Limited Express HARUKA (reserved seats up to 2 times)
- Local, Rapid, Special Rapid trains within the area
- Sagano Line, Nara Line, Kosei Line (to Omi-Maiko), Biwako Line (to at least Hikone), Osaka Loop Line, Yumesaki Line
- JR West local buses (Kyoto Station–Takao–Shuzan)
- Does NOT cover Shinkansen or other limited express trains

**Key restriction:** Shinkansen completely excluded. Only Haruka is covered among limited express trains.

**Boundary stations (approximate):**
- Kosei Line: up to Omi-Maiko (Hieizan-Sakamoto is covered)
- Biwako/Tokaido Line: extends to Hikone (Omi-Hachiman is covered)
- Sagano/San-in Line: to Sonobe/Kameoka area
- Nara Line: full coverage to Nara

### Option B: JR West Kansai WIDE Area Pass

| Duration | Adult | Child |
|----------|-------|-------|
| 5-day (consecutive) | ¥12,000 | ¥6,000 |

**Coverage:**
- Everything in the Kansai Area Pass PLUS:
- Sanyo Shinkansen (Shin-Osaka ↔ Okayama), reserved seats included

- Extends to Okayama, Kurashiki, Tottori, Kinosaki Onsen, Takamatsu (Shikoku)

**Assessment for your trip:** At ¥12,000 for 5 days, this is overkill unless you plan day trips to Okayama/Kinosaki. Your JR usage totals ~¥9,300, so this pass would NOT save money.

## 3. Recommendation: Best Pass Strategy

### Recommended approach: HARUKA One-Way Tickets + ICOCA (pay-as-you-go)

**Reasoning:**

Your JR usage is spread across 9 days but is relatively light on most days. No single multi-day JR pass covers the full trip economically.

| Strategy | Cost |
|----------|------|
| All individual tickets (no discount) | ~¥9,300 |
| 2x Haruka One-Way (¥1,800 each) + individual local fares | ~¥6,460 |
| 1-day Kansai Area Pass (Jun 19 arrival day) + 1-day (Jun 23 Omi-Hachiman) + individual rest | Complex, marginal savings |

**Best strategy breakdown:**
1. **Jun 19 (KIX→Kyoto):** Buy HARUKA One-Way Ticket = ¥1,800
2. **Jun 20 (Arashiyama):** Pay ¥480 round trip (IC card)
3. **Jun 22 (Hieizan-Sakamoto):** Pay ¥660 round trip (IC card)
4. **Jun 23 (Omi-Hachiman):** Pay ¥1,360 round trip (IC card)
5. **Jun 24 (Uji→Osaka):** Pay ~¥800 (IC card)
6. **Jun 25 (Universal City):** Pay ¥360 round trip (IC card)
7. **Jun 27 (Osaka→KIX):** Buy HARUKA One-Way Ticket = ¥1,300-1,800

**Total with Haruka discount: approximately ¥6,460-¥6,960**
**Savings vs no discount: ¥2,340-¥2,840**

### Alternative: 1-day Kansai Area Pass on June 23

If you use a 1-day Kansai Area Pass (¥2,700) on June 23 for the Omi-Hachiman trip, it would cost MORE than the ¥1,360 round trip fare. The 1-day pass only makes sense if you combine multiple JR trips in one day.

**Verdict: The 1-day pass is NOT worth it for any single day in your itinerary.**

## 4. Non-JR Passes & Cards

### KANSAI ONE PASS (IC Card)

- **Price:** ¥3,000 (includes ¥500 deposit, ¥2,500 usable balance)
- **What it is:** An ICOCA-based IC card for tourists with special facility discounts
- **Works on:** All trains and buses accepting ICOCA/IC cards (JR, private railways, subways, buses)
- **Benefits:** Discounts at tourist facilities, works everywhere
- **Available at:** Kansai Airport, major stations
- **Note:** Facility benefits end March 2027

### Regular ICOCA Card
- **Price:** ¥2,000 (includes ¥500 deposit, ¥1,500 usable balance)
- **Recommendation:** Either ICOCA or KANSAI ONE PASS works. The ONE PASS gives ¥1,000 more initial balance for ¥1,000 more cost, plus facility discounts.

---

### Kyoto Subway & Bus 1-Day Pass

- **Price:** ¥1,100 adult / ¥550 child
- **Coverage:** Kyoto City Subway + City Bus + Kyoto Bus + Keihan Bus + West Japan JR Bus (limited routes)
- **Note:** The old ¥700 bus-only day pass was discontinued in 2024
- **Worth it on Jun 21 (Ohara day):** Bus to Ohara costs ¥580 one-way (¥1,160 round trip). If you also take buses within Kyoto that day, the ¥1,100 pass pays for itself.

## 5. Summary: Optimal Strategy

### Total estimated transportation cost:

| Item | Cost |
|------|------|
| HARUKA One-Way: KIX→Kyoto | ¥1,800 |
| Jun 20: JR Kyoto→Arashiyama round trip | ¥480 |
| Jun 21: Bus to Ohara (round trip) | ¥1,160 |
| Jun 21: Eizan Railway to Kifune (round trip) | ¥840 |
| Jun 22: JR Kyoto→Hieizan-Sakamoto round trip | ¥660 |
| Jun 23: JR Kyoto→Omi-Hachiman round trip | ¥1,360 |
| Jun 24: JR Kyoto→Uji + Uji→Osaka | ¥800 |
| Jun 25: JR Osaka→Universal City round trip | ¥360 |
| Jun 27: HARUKA One-Way: Osaka→KIX | ¥1,800 |
| **TOTAL** | **¥9,260** |

### Passes to buy:
1. **HARUKA One-Way Ticket x2** (KIX→Kyoto ¥1,800 + Osaka→KIX ¥1,800) - saves ~¥2,440 vs regular fares
2. **ICOCA or KANSAI ONE PASS** - for all other pay-as-you-go rides
3. **Kyoto Subway & Bus 1-Day Pass** (¥1,100) - on Jun 21 IF taking 3+ bus rides that day (Ohara round trip + local buses)
4. **Consider Keihan Railway for Jun 24** (Uji→Osaka via Keihan: ~¥490 vs JR ¥800)

### Passes NOT worth buying:
- JR Kansai Area Pass (any duration) - daily JR usage too low
- JR Kansai Wide Area Pass - way too expensive for this itinerary
- Kansai Railway Pass Lite - expensive and doesn't cover JR
- Eizan Ee Kippu - unless visiting both Kifune AND Kurama

### Key money-saving tips:
1. Always buy HARUKA One-Way tourist tickets (saves ¥2,440 total)
2. Use Keihan Railway for Uji→Osaka on Jun 24 (saves ~¥310 vs JR)
3. Consider Nankai instead of Haruka for Osaka→KIX on Jun 27 if staying near Namba (saves ¥830)
4. Get the Subway & Bus pass on Ohara day only if taking 3+ bus rides
